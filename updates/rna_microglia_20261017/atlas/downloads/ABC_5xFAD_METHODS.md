# Microglia 5xFAD versus WT_3mo: ABC analysis

## Results and files

The upstream ABC model was run with the user's Microglia ATAC peaks, direct per-mouse BAM measurements of candidates and 1 kb promoters, and a common candidate set for WT_3mo and 5xFAD (four mice per group). Upstream source commit: 92ac50360231a6bcfd654f3147839d078be445d9. GENCODE vM25/mm10, 21,859 protein-coding genes with one APPRIS-supported transcript per gene; chr1–19 and chrX in predictions.

The standard candidate construction starts from 500 bp summit windows, merges overlapping windows and adds measured promoters. It produced 73,316 final regions, median width 819 bp, with 3,015 above 2 kb. The resulting genome-wide comparison contains 4,983,667 TE-overlapping peak–gene rows. The separate fixed-width sensitivity has 68,376 exactly 500 bp non-overlapping peak windows plus 20,663 merged promoter regions; 5,114,847 TE-overlapping peak–gene rows. Its candidate construction is a sensitivity analysis, not the unmodified upstream candidate builder; its downstream score calculation uses the upstream model.

Full results are on LiSC under `/lisc/data/scratch/molecular_neurosciences/i-med/p5_Glial_TE_regulation_aging/ABC_standard_microglia_20261114/`, in `standard_5xFAD_results_v1/` and `fixed_5xFAD_results_v1/`. Each has `5xFAD_vs_WT3_TE_candidate_gene_all.tsv.gz`, ranked genes, Myo1e results, per-mouse promoter data and PDF figures. The TE-copy annotations and IRescue RNA measurements are linked via `candidate_TE_mapping_v1/` and `candidate_TE_mapping_fixed_v1/`, respectively; check the directory listing for the mapping filenames. The full gzipped tables passed gzip integrity checks in LiSC job 8000403.

For example, the fixed-width Lgals9 candidate chr11:78982755-78983255 has a 5xFAD ABC score of 0.489059 versus 0.279977 in WT_3mo. This is a selected descriptive example, not evidence of statistical significance or a causal link. The existing mouse-level gene-expression analysis is joined separately. Per-copy TE RNA values are sums of separate mouse IRescue expected allocations; they are not independently measured autonomous TE transcripts.

## Checks and interpretation

Independent reconstruction matched 56 fixed-width scores, including 16 self-promoters, within 4.94e-7 (scores are exported to six decimal places). Among 767,888 exactly matching 500 bp TE–gene pairs with positive 5xFAD TE RNA, 33,080 had opposite score-change directions between the standard and fixed candidate universes. These links are not independent biological replicates. On 120 selected fixed-width links, 3 changed direction between K562-reference quantile normalization and a no-quantile-normalization sensitivity; these are not a genome-wide estimate. Direct sampled all-versus-no-secondary checks were run for 128 candidates per model in each of eight mice; they are not a full ABC refit.

The upstream model's K562 quantile-normalization reference is human and not validated for this mouse dataset. Activity is ATAC-only; contact uses the published power-law model, not measured 5xFAD Hi-C; H3K27ac was not measured. Upstream nofilter BAM counting includes secondary alignments. BAMs have no duplicate flags, which does not prove deduplication. The upstream code doubles chrX/Y counts for both sexes; chrX predictions are flagged and chrY was excluded from prediction. Reference chrY promoter alignments were observed in female-labelled BAMs, mainly secondary; this is not evidence of a female Y chromosome and its cause is unresolved. The 370,514 peak records include 14,893 exact duplicate rows with malformed group fields; independent replay without those rows produced byte-identical standard candidate BED coordinates.

ABC scores are relative within a target-gene candidate universe. Condition score changes are descriptive and have no link-level p-value or FDR in this output. The official promoter-activity expression proxy is not RNA expression, and its pass/fail outputs were both retained. Avoid causal conclusions or treating predicted contacts as directly observed interactions.
