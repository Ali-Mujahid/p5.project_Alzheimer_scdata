# Microglia group peak–TE coordinate trends

Source: five pooled Microglia group narrowPeak files in `/lisc/data/scratch/molecular_neurosciences/i-med/p5_Glial_TE_regulation_aging/te-atac`; canonical mm10 RepeatMasker table in `references/mm10/canonical.rmsk.txt`. LiSC compute job 7596773 completed 0:0 and generated the two TSVs. Source script: `pre_processed_ATAC/analysis_source/ATAC_TE_group_peak_trends_20261022_v2/group_peak_te_trends.py` (SHA-256 `75e09f5f27965ea59c1481716b75ba65ddfbb6384896c6f071940bf7b69665db`).

For each narrowPeak, the summit is `chromStart + peakOffset`. A peak summit is TE-overlapping when it falls in at least one eligible RepeatMasker interval. Exact duplicate TE-copy records keyed by chromosome, start, end, strand and repName are counted once. Eligible classes: DNA, LINE, LTR, RC, Retroposon and SINE; uncertain `?` annotations are excluded. The total endpoint is the fraction of peak summits inside these eligible TE intervals. The subfamily endpoint counts TE copies touched by at least one peak summit and divides by `all touched eligible TE copies × subfamily reference copies / all eligible reference copies`. Subfamilies are displayed only when their full mm10 reference has **strictly more than 2,000 copies**; 233 pass.

The four distinct requested comparisons are WT_3mo versus WT_6mo, WT_21mo, 5xFAD_3mo and APOE4_3mo. Table differences are the comparison group minus WT_3mo. The repeated WT_21mo entry was included once. `APOE4_3mo` is the filename label for the APOE4/TREM2 condition.

These are pooled group peak-coordinate associations, not read-level ATAC accessibility, per-mouse tests, FDR-controlled differential results, TE activity, or RNA expression. The all-TE-copy background does not match mappability or GC and TE-copy observations are correlated. Peak caller sensitivity and peak counts differ between group files. For differential inference use per-animal peak/fragment counts on a common peak set with mice as replicates.
