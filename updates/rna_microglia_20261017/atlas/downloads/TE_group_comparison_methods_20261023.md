# TE-bound peaks and disease-rising TE trends

Our five group peak sets are pooled across four mice per condition; each peak summit is tested against the canonical mm10 RepeatMasker TE locus set. The published processed peak matrix supplies separately thresholded peak features for four Microglia mice per group; a feature counts if its full interval overlaps a TE. The published plot displays the arithmetic mean of four mouse fractions; the counts table sums mouse-peak observations, not unique loci. These endpoints, peak widths, and cell selections differ, so the percentages are descriptive and not a like-for-like performance ranking.

Genomic context uses the previously reviewed Figure 2 B table: the lower midpoint of each TE-positive peak was assigned one exclusive gene context. The source sets include all annotated cell types, not Microglia-specific peaks; they show architecture, not expression.

The disease-rising heatmap contains 12 subfamilies out of 233 with more than 2,000 annotated copies. Selection rule: WT_3mo observed/expected < 1 and both 5xFAD_3mo and APOE4_3mo observed/expected > 1. All five groups are displayed. This is a descriptive screen of pooled group peaks. It is not mouse-level differential accessibility, FDR-tested enrichment, or RNA TE expression. Background copies were not matched for mappability or GC; nearby TE loci may be correlated.
